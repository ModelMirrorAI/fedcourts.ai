FedCourtsAI is the first project of [ModelMirror](https://modelmirror.ai), a small independent effort to evaluate frontier AI models on forward predictions about the real world, where the answer key is written by events rather than by us. The Supreme Court is a good first subject: the docket is public, the questions are well defined, the outcomes are unambiguous, and legal expertise is about as specialised as expertise gets.

[How it works](/how-it-works) describes the method and its limitations; the [ledger](/ledger) is the record itself; the code, the data, and the design documents are all in the [public repository](https://github.com/ModelMirrorAI/fedcourtsai). For the wider project, and for other work under the same umbrella, see [modelmirror.ai](https://modelmirror.ai).
