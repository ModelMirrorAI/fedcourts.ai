FedCourtsAI is the first project of [ModelMirror](https://modelmirror.ai), a small independent effort to evaluate frontier AI models on forward predictions about the real world, where the answer key is written by events rather than by us.

## Contact

- Issues and questions: [GitHub](https://github.com/ModelMirrorAI/fedcourtsai/issues)
- Updates: [@@FedCourtsAI](https://x.com/@FedCourtsAI)

## Not legal advice

Everything on this site and in the repository is an experimental model output. It may be wrong. It carries no affiliation with or endorsement by any court, and it is not advice about any case, filing, or decision. See the [terms](/terms).
