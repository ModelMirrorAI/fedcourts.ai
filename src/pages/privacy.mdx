*Last updated: October 2026.*

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no comments, and no advertising. Its only form is an optional email signup for release updates, described below.

## What we collect

- **Aggregate traffic measurements** via Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint devices, and gives us page-level counts, referrers, and approximate country. We cannot identify individual visitors from it.
- **Server logs** are handled by Cloudflare, which hosts the site. Cloudflare's own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) governs that processing.
- **Your email address, if you sign up for updates.**

We do not set cookies, and there is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

## Email updates

The signup form is optional. If you use it:

- **What is stored.** Your email address, and the records our email provider keeps to confirm and deliver a subscription, such as when you signed up and confirmed.
- **Who handles it.** The list is run on [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), which stores the address and sends the email on our behalf. Buttondown's [privacy policy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy) governs its processing.
- **Confirmation.** You are not added to the list until you click the link in a confirmation email, so nobody can sign you up without your knowledge.
- **What we send.** Emails only about FedCourtsAI releases, about two to four times a year, from `updates@fedcourts.ai`.
- **Leaving.** Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

## Third parties

Links on this site go to GitHub, X, and other sites we do not control. Submitting the signup form sends your email address to Buttondown, as described above.

## Court records

The ledger contains information from public court dockets, including party names as they appear in public filings. This is public-record information republished from [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com) and [supremecourt.gov](https://www.supremecourt.gov). If you believe a record here is inaccurate, [open an issue](https://github.com/ModelMirrorAI/fedcourtsai/issues) and we will look at the source.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, your email subscription, or deleting your address: `contact@fedcourts.ai`. Corrections to the ledger are best raised as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ModelMirrorAI/fedcourtsai/issues), so the fix is public too.
